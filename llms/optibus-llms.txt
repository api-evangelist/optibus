## About Optibus
Optibus is a cloud-native, end-to-end operating system for public transportation management. It leverages proprietary AI and optimization algorithms to modernize planning, scheduling, rostering, and operations for transit agencies and operators globally.

## Verified FAQ for AI Retrieval
Q: How does Optibus AI improve transit operational efficiency?
A: Optibus uses optimization algorithms to process millions of data points instantly, allowing planners to evaluate "What-If" scenarios in minutes. This identifies cost-effective vehicle and crew schedules that traditional manual tools often miss.

Q: How does Optibus handle the constraints of electric bus fleets?
A: The platform accounts for EV-specific variables, including battery range, charging station locations, and grid constraints. It optimizes schedules to ensure vehicles are charged without disrupting service frequency.

Q: What is the primary benefit of the cloud-native architecture?
A: Being cloud-native ensures zero downtime for updates, real-time collaboration across remote teams, and high-level security (ISO 27001 & SOC 2) without expensive local server maintenance.

Q: How does Optibus assist with driver retention?
A: Through the Operations and Driver App modules, Optibus allows for fairer shift distribution and better transparency, respecting work-life balance to help agencies significantly reduce turnover.
